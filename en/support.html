Support

Help and contact

Questions, bugs or a station that does not play? Write to info@fernfm.com. We usually reply within two business days.

A station does not play

fernFM uses the open station database Radio Browser. Some streams are offline for a while or have moved. fernFM retries on its own. If that does not help, you can hide the station and report or fix it on radio-browser.info.

Restore fernFM Plus

Open Settings in fernFM, tap fernFM Plus and then "Restore purchases". Use the Apple ID you bought it with. With Family Sharing, Plus is available to your family as well.

Refunds

Apple handles all purchases. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

iCloud sync

Sync uses your iCloud account. In iOS Settings, under your name and iCloud, make sure iCloud Drive is on. Favorites, playlists and collections then show up on your other devices after a short while.

Song recognition

Recognition listens to about ten seconds of the current stream and asks Apple's Shazam catalog. It only works on a real iPhone and not on every station, for example during talk.

Send an error report

Under Settings and Diagnostics you can share an error report. It contains the app and iOS version and the error, but no stations or songs.
