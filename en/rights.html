Rights holders and complaints

Last updated: November 21, 2026

fernFM does not broadcast any content of its own. The app shows stations from the open Radio-Browser database and plays their streams directly from the station's server. We do not pick or feature any stations: lists like "Popular" are generated automatically from the database's play counts.

What we do

If a station infringes your rights (for example copyright or trademark) or distributes illegal content, we add it to our central blocklist. Blocked stations disappear for all users from search, lists, favorites, history and playlists, and can no longer be played. The app loads the list at launch and then at least every six hours.

How to report a station

In the app: long-press a station or open the menu in the player, then "Report a rights or content issue". Or email info@fernfm.com with:

  • the station's name and, if possible, the stream address or Radio-Browser ID
  • which right is infringed or which content is illegal, with a short reason
  • your name and, if you act for a rights holder, on whose behalf
  • a statement that your information is accurate to the best of your knowledge

How fast

We usually confirm receipt within two business days and review every report. If the notice is valid or clearly justified, we block the station without delay; for all users the block takes effect at the latest when the app next loads the list. We will tell you the outcome.

Counter-notice

If you run a blocked station and believe the block is wrong, write to the same address with your reasons. We review it and lift the block if it is justified.

Errors in a station entry

A wrong name, wrong country or a dead stream is not a legal issue. You can correct such entries directly at radio-browser.info; in the app you can also hide a station for yourself.
