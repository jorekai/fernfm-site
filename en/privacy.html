Privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

In short: fernFM has no server of its own, no account, no ads of its own and no tracking. Your favorites, playlists and songs stay on your device and, if you use fernFM Plus, in your own iCloud account. We see none of it.

2. Data on your device

Favorites, station playlists, hidden stations, the song history, saved songs, collections and error reports are stored locally in the app. They only leave the device in the cases described below. You can delete all data in Settings; deleting the app removes it as well.

3. Services the app talks to directly

For these connections your device necessarily sends its IP address to the provider. We do not receive this data.

  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info): station lists, search and station details. Your search terms and filters are sent. When you play a station, its ID is reported so the database can count popular stations.
  • Radio stations: the audio stream and song titles come straight from the station's server. Station logos load from the address the station provides.
  • Apple iTunes Search: for recognized songs, artist and title are sent to Apple to find artwork, a preview and a link to Apple Music.
  • Apple (previews): when you play a preview, your device loads the 30-second clip straight from Apple's servers.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA): At launch and then every six hours, the app loads our blocklist (blocklist.json) from this website. It contains the IDs or server addresses of blocked stations, the type of complaint and the date, but nothing about reporters or users. Your device sends nothing beyond the technically necessary request.

4. Stations near you (location)

Only when you tap "Stations near me" and allow access does fernFM read an approximate location once. It is rounded to about one kilometer and sent to Radio Browser for this search only. fernFM does not store the location and does not use it in the background. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw in your device settings at any time.

5. Song recognition (ShazamKit)

When you start song recognition, fernFM creates an audio signature from about ten seconds of the current stream and sends it to Apple's Shazam service. The microphone is not used. Apple's privacy policy applies.

6. iCloud sync (fernFM Plus)

With fernFM Plus, favorites, playlists, saved songs and collections are stored in the iCloud key-value store of your own iCloud account. Apple processes this data as the provider of iCloud. We have no access to it.

7. Apple Music (export and sync)

When you export a collection as a playlist and allow access, fernFM uses MusicKit to create a playlist in your Apple Music library.

If you turn on Apple Music sync with fernFM Plus, fernFM automatically adds newly saved songs to the playlist "fernFM" in your library, and optionally to one playlist per station. What is sent are the Apple Music IDs of these songs and the playlist names (for per-station playlists, the station name). You can turn sync off in Settings at any time and withdraw access in your device settings.

8. Purchases

fernFM Plus is bought through the App Store, or through Google Play on Android devices. Apple or Google handles payment. The app verifies the purchase on the device through StoreKit or Google Play Billing. We receive no payment data.

9. Error reports

Error reports stay on the device. They contain the app and system version and the error, but no stations or songs. A report is only sent if you explicitly share it, through the channel you choose.

10. No analytics, no ads

fernFM contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs and builds no user profiles.

11. This website

This site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs. The site sets no cookies and loads no external fonts or trackers.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since we store no personal data about you, most requests can be handled directly in the app. Feel free to write to info@fernfm.com.
